The Hourglass City

New Meridian, 1957

Noir pixel-art games that run in your browser. Every building, car, person, raindrop and letter is drawn in code, and the sound effects and music are synthesized as you play. There are no libraries, no image files and no audio files: each game is a single HTML page.
